# AI Agent Guide

Work on KEV as an evidence-constrained research system, not as a chatbot or an
AGI project. Preserve the current stack: neural language proposals, symbolic
typed state, bounded receipt-backed action, and gated evolution.

## Authority boundaries

The completed clause-local v1 adapter is non-activating research, not the
production inference path. See `docs/CLAUSE_LOCAL_V1.md`: the stronger no-model
control scored better. Preserve the negative attribution conclusion and carry
the exposed research pack into exclusions before any later training campaign.

- Neural output may propose frame kinds and cardinality; parser-bound exact
  slots decide what can enter state.
- A frozen encoder supplies a fixed feature interface. It is not cognition, and
  its presence or training curve does not establish broader capability.
- Language observations are `REPORTED`. A tool result becomes `VERIFIED` only
  through the receipt-first action path.
- Plans read only facts, goals, constraints, observations, predictions, and
  reviewed lessons. Do not turn prose into a hidden command language.
- The default tool registry is empty. Tools require declarations, schemas, and
  an explicit allowlist; production mutation is forbidden.
- Facts use revision records and supersedes pointers. Do not collapse conflicts
  or discard earlier evidence.
- Lesson entry creates a `DRAFT`. Only explicit review with reviewer, time, and
  permission provenance permits export for training.
- A language surface may narrate explicit state and receipts. Other generated
  claims must be marked `UNGROUNDED` and receive no fact-write authority.

## Model-change protocol

1. Verify the current incumbent and frozen artifacts with `kev doctor`.
2. Export only reviewed lessons and confirm no promotion-suite or held-out-
   vocabulary leakage.
3. Train a new immutable challenger from the recorded parent hash.
4. Preserve the uncalibrated checkpoint; fit temperature only on the separate
   calibration slice and write a new calibrated artifact.
5. Evaluate incumbent and challenger on the same frozen fresh, retention, OOV,
   composition, and calibration splits.
6. Qualify only on strict fresh and composition improvement, retention within
   epsilon, no OOV regression, and no regression in any reported audit family.
   A tie rejects.
7. Keep training loss out of promotion, and retain every candidate, failure,
   receipt, eval card, and ledger decision.

Do not refresh an item after seeing its result. Version a new suite and carry
the old suite and report forward as development evidence. Do not merge Cortex,
auto-train on chat, build a local RAG chatbot, or describe KEV as AGI or
superintelligence.

permits repeated kinds. Ambiguous matches and insufficient cardinality abstain;
